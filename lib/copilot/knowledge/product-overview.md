# Orixs Product Overview

Orixs is an AI-assisted, multi-tenant business operations platform for organizations that want one place to manage work instead of juggling many disconnected tools.

The site positions Orixs as an "AI Copilot for Business Excellence" and "your AI Copilot for business automation." It brings together CRM, HR, payroll, recruitment, finance, projects, documents, commerce, communications, reporting, travel planning, support, and related operations into configurable workspaces.

Orixs is designed for industry-specific deployments:

- Business Operations for general companies and teams.
- Insurance for claims, underwriting, policy workflows, customer self-service, and compliance.
- Construction for projects, crews, budgets, BIM-related planning, procurement, safety, and site visibility.
- Intern Africa for internship and talent pipeline workflows.
- Scene One for media, studio, casting, booking, contracts, and production operations.

The public website is the marketing and signup surface. It explains use cases, resources, case studies, pricing, and signup flows. The Copilot can guide users on this website, but it does not operate inside the separate workspace apps.

Company context:

- Orixs is presented as part of Alcor Group.
- The footer says: "AI Copilot for Business Excellence by ALCOR GROUP."
- Publicly listed locations include India, South Africa, USA, KSA, and Singapore. The About page includes India and South Africa office addresses.
- Leadership shown on the About page: Rakesh Kasa, Mahesh Kumar Baheti, M Abdul Raoof, Dr. Lizo Mkhutshulwa, and Mahesh T. Kotecha.

Important boundaries:

- The Copilot can answer questions from public site content and live public plan data.
- The Copilot cannot access private workspace data, customer records, payment data, admin systems, source code, credentials, or infrastructure details.
- The Copilot cannot submit signup forms, complete payments, or modify account data. It may navigate users to the correct public page.
